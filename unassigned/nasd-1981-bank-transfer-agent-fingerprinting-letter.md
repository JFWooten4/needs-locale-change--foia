# SEC FOIA request — October 5, 1981 NASD bank transfer-agent fingerprinting letter

Please provide an electronic copy of the **October 5, 1981 letter from John T. Wall, Executive Vice President, Member and Market Services, National Association of Securities Dealers (NASD), to Marc L. Weinberg, Esq., of the Securities and Exchange Commission staff**, concerning NASD's interpretation of its fingerprinting plan to extend fingerprint-card submission services to registered bank transfer agents.

Please include any enclosures, attachments, exhibits, cover letters, routing or transmittal sheets, or annotations retained with this specifically identified letter. This request is limited to that letter and its directly associated materials, **not** the full historical fingerprinting-program file.

Please search the Division of Market Regulation's records concerning Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-18170 (*Fingerprinting Program*), its related fingerprinting-plan interpretations, and any associated SEC archival or public-reference files. If the record is available only on microfiche, microfilm, retired paper, Federal Records Center/offsite storage, or legacy Public Reference Room holdings, please search those holdings and provide the best available reproduction.

## Source identifying the record

The SEC's notice in [Securities Exchange Act Release No. 34-18170, 46 FR 51692–51693 (October 21, 1981)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1981-10-21/pdf/FR-1981-10-21.pdf#page=149) identifies the October 5, 1981 Wall-to-Weinberg letter in a footnote supporting the Commission's statement that NASD had confirmed it would extend fingerprint-card services to registered bank transfer agents under its existing plan. The notice states an effective date of October 13, 1981.

This letter is particularly relevant to distinguishing NASD's existing fingerprinting services for **non-bank transfer agents** from the **1981 extension to bank transfer agents**.

Consistent with the SEC's recent classification of WhyDRS as a Media requester, these records are sought for source-linked public research and editorial analysis and will be disseminated freely to the public, not for commercial use. I request treatment as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR § 200.80(g)(12), because this contemporaneous interpretation documents the origin and administration of SRO fingerprinting services for registered bank transfer agents.
